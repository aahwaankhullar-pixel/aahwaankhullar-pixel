## Aahwaan Khullar

Student researcher and developer from Delhi, India (Shiv Nadar School, Faridabad).
I use data to understand how systems treat the people they are meant to serve: how a government programme is described, what official numbers miss, who gets left out of everyday technology, and how prices and demand behave in real markets.

### Research

| Project | What it asks | Methods |
|---|---|---|
| [**Stakeholder framing in the Swachh Bharat Mission**](https://github.com/aahwaankhullar-pixel/sbm-analysis) | Does the way India's sanitation mission is written about depend on who is writing and who is written about? | Python · NLTK · VADER sentiment · LDA topic modelling · Mann–Whitney U · 55-document corpus |
| [**Declared vs. measured**](https://github.com/aahwaankhullar-pixel/declared-vs-measured) | Every state declared itself "open defecation free". What did the national health survey find in 704 districts? | pandas · PDF data extraction · first-difference regression with clustered standard errors |
| **From Accessibility to Prediction** | How can usage data from an accessible app help design adaptive digital interventions for neurodiverse children? | Data from the GroovAbility pilot (42 children) · in progress |
| **Data-driven cybersecurity risk assessment in FinTech** | Which factors drive the frequency and cost of data breaches in financial technology? | Python · statistics · structured breach datasets |
